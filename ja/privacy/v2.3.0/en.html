Daily Japanese · Privacy Policy

Applies to the Android / iOS / HarmonyOS versions of “Daily Japanese” (package name com.github.boybeak.daily.lang.ja)
Version v2.3.0 · Effective date：2026-10-22

In one sentence: this app does not collect your personal information, and it does not require an account.

  • No recording. Listening and reading practice only plays audio — the microphone is never opened and your voice is never captured.
  • No uploads. Learning progress and quiz records stay on your own device.
  • No sharing. No ads, no analytics SDK, and no third party receives your data.

1.What data we process

  • Learning progress (what you have studied, most recent study time): processed — stored only on your device (see section 3).
  • Quiz records (right / wrong answers in end-of-lesson quizzes): processed — stored only on your device (see section 3).
  • Learning preferences (interface language, daily goal, etc.): processed — stored only on your device (see section 3).
  • Purchase entitlements (owned courses and unlock status): processed — stored only on your device (see sections 3 and 4).
  • Microphone recordings, audio: not processed — no microphone permission is requested; nothing is recorded or uploaded (see section 5).
  • Account, phone number, email: not processed — the current version has no sign-in or account system.
  • Device identifiers, location, contacts, photo library, clipboard: not processed — not read, not collected.
  • Crash logs, usage statistics: not processed (device-local logs only) — the current version has no reporting backend (see section 6).

2.What system permissions the app requests

The current version requests only the network permission (Android's android.permission.INTERNET), reserved for possible future content updates. Apart from that, it requests no sensitive permissions such as camera, microphone, location, contacts, photo library or notifications. The clipboard and device identifiers are likewise never read.

3.Data stored on your device

  • What is stored: which items you have studied and when you last studied, the per-question right/wrong records of end-of-lesson quizzes, and your interface language and learning preferences (such as the daily goal), and purchase entitlements (owned courses and unlock status).
  • Where it is stored: only on your device, inside the app's private storage. It is never uploaded to any server.
  • How to delete it: uninstall the app, or use "Clear app data" in your system settings, and these records are deleted. The app offers no cloud sync, so deleted records cannot be recovered.
  • Not used to identify you: these records describe "the learning activity on this device"; they contain nothing that points to your personal identity.

4.Purchases, subscriptions and payments

  • Advanced courses can be unlocked in two ways: a one-time purchase, or an auto-renewing subscription (membership). Payments for purchases and subscriptions are handled by the store billing service of the channel you use — Google Play Billing on Android, HUAWEI IAP Kit on HarmonyOS, and App Store in-app purchases on iOS. Payment details such as card numbers are processed by the respective store; we never see or store any payment information.
  • Subscriptions renew automatically: unless cancelled, they renew at the end of the current billing period. You can cancel at any time in the store's subscription management — "Payments & subscriptions" in Google Play on Android, "Payments & purchases" under your HUAWEI ID on HarmonyOS, and "Settings › Apple ID › Subscriptions" on iOS. After cancelling, your entitlement remains until the end of the current period.
  • Purchase entitlements (unlocked courses and subscription status) are stored locally on your device only, so the app can restore your owned content the next time you open it; they contain nothing that can identify you personally.
  • After reinstalling or switching devices, owned content is restored from the purchase history of the respective store — no account required.
  • Refunds follow the refund policy of the respective store.

5.Microphone and audio

  • The current version does not request the microphone permission and does not record or upload any audio: no audio-recording permission exists in the app's permission list.
  • Listening and reading practice only plays audio: the practice screen plays learning audio bundled with the app; it never opens the microphone or captures your voice.
  • Repeat-after-me recording and pronunciation scoring are outside the current version's feature set. If a future version adds them, we will update this policy before that version ships, and will not request the microphone permission before then.

6.Analytics, crashes and logs

  • In-app event markers (used to tell apart apps for different target languages) are currently written only to device-local logs, for development troubleshooting, and are not uploaded.
  • The current version integrates no analytics, advertising or crash-reporting service.
  • If such services are added in the future, this policy will be updated accordingly, and the updated version will describe the scope of collection.

7.Third-party components and data sharing

  • The app does not sell, rent or share any of your data with third parties (in fact, we have no such data to share).
  • The app integrates no advertising, profiling or data-analytics SDKs.
  • Interface rendering and audio playback use open-source components; inside the app these components only draw the interface and play the bundled audio, and they do not collect your data.
  • The learning content and audio bundled with the app are produced by us and do not involve your personal information.
  • In-app purchases are completed through the store billing service of your platform (Google Play Billing on Android, HUAWEI IAP Kit on HarmonyOS, and App Store in-app purchases on iOS); the processing of payment information is governed by the privacy policy of the respective store operator.

8.Minors

This app is not designed for children, and it does not knowingly collect any personal information from children. Because the app collects no personal information and requires no account, children using it under a parent's guidance do not generate personal data as a result.

9.Changes to this policy and contact

If this policy changes, we will update it at the same time as we release the new version, and we will update the effective date. For material changes to data handling, we will give a prominent notice in the app.

If you have questions about this policy, or wish to know about or delete data related to you (that data exists only on your device — clearing the app's data is enough), please contact us at the email above.

This policy describes the actual behaviour of the current app version, not a promise about future versions. If the feature set changes, this policy will be updated accordingly.
