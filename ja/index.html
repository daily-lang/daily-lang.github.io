怎么学 HOW IT WORKS

「听 → 读」短练习 Listen → read sessions

与每日越南语同一套学法：短练习、固定节奏，句子都是日常里用得上的。日语特有：从五十音的「逐拍点读」起步，假名注音 + 逐拍发音，重音与拍感听得见。 The same approach as Daily Viet: short sessions with a fixed rhythm, using phrases you can actually use. Japanese-specific: it starts from kana with tap-by-mora playback, kana readings on kanji, and per-mora audio so pitch accent and mora timing are audible.

进度只存本机 Progress stays on device

不需要注册账号，学习进度与偏好保存在你自己的设备上；没有广告，也没有统计上报。 No account needed. Progress and preferences are stored on your own device — no ads, no analytics.
