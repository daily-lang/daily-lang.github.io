怎么学 HOW IT WORKS

「听 → 读」短练习 Listen → read sessions

每课按固定节奏推进：热身对话 → 词块 → 发音对比 → 例句 → 语法。一次练一点，句子都是日常里用得上的。 Every lesson follows the same rhythm: warm-up dialogue → chunks → pronunciation contrast → sentences → grammar. A little at a time, with phrases you can actually use.

发音专项 Pronunciation focus

越南语六个声调逐组过关：每课一个焦点音，配上最小对比组，先听清差别，再开口模仿。 Work through the six Vietnamese tones group by group: one focus sound per lesson with minimal contrast pairs — hear the difference first, then say it aloud.

课后小测 Lesson quiz

每课结束用小测检验听读；答错的句子集中列在一起，点一下就能重听。 A short quiz checks your listening and reading at the end of each lesson. Missed items are listed together — tap one to hear it again.

进度只存本机 Progress stays on device

不需要注册账号，学习进度与偏好保存在你自己的设备上；没有广告，也没有统计上报。 No account needed. Progress and preferences are stored on your own device — no ads, no analytics.
